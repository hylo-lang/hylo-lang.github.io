Grammars are sourced from the following repositories:
- EBNF: https://github.com/Alhadis/language-grammars
- Hylo: https://github.com/hylo-lang/vscode-hylo
- Hylo IR and WebAssembly assembly (`hylo-ir`, `wasm-asm`): maintained here; see `README.md`

The playground's editor settings for Hylo (comments, brackets, and the characters closed or
wrapped as you type), `hylo.language-configuration.json`, are vscode-hylo's
`language-configuration.json` (Apache-2.0), as of
[`134c148`](https://github.com/hylo-lang/vscode-hylo/blob/134c1483f0aee9d53e796be1282101b5205bfb5e/language-configuration.json),
without its one comment, so that it is plain JSON.
